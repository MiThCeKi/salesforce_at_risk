Tracks any SiftMed account with a non-zero Annual_Contract_Value__c, a non-zero page cap (PageCountCap__c), both contract dates on file, and an assigned Account_Tier__c — regardless of Salesforce pipeline stage (Customer, Previous Customer, SQL, Prospect, Unqualifed, Kick Back, etc. all qualify if the data's there). This deliberately has no Stage__c filter: the criterion is whether an account has the data to compute a usage %, not which stage it currently carries, so already-churned (Previous Customer) and still-selling (SQL, Prospect) accounts appear alongside signed customers. Usage compares trailing 30-day product usage (Pages_Last_30__c) to the contracted or projected page cap, prorated to each account's actual contract length (Active_Contract_Start_Date__c to Subscription_End_Date__c) rather than a flat 12-month assumption — the same usage-cliff pattern that preceded all nine of SiftMed's confirmed churns. Accounts missing a start date, end date, or page cap, or with a zero page cap or a negative contract length, are excluded from the calculation (shown as N/A) but still listed. Non-real and internal Account records (null or zero Annual_Contract_Value__c), and accounts with no Account_Tier__c assigned, are excluded from the 77 tracked-account base entirely — except for a small manually-maintained list of accounts the team has asked to always track regardless of data completeness. The Stage column shows the account's tier alongside a Customer-stage account, e.g. “Customer (Enterprise)”; every other stage shows just the stage name — those accounts haven't necessarily signed a contract yet (or, for Previous Customer, no longer have one), so their page cap and usage figures may be provisional or stale. Last Email reflects the most recent logged Email-type Task on the Account, with the sending rep shown beneath the date when known; Next Meeting reflects the earliest future-dated Event on the Account — both show “Never” / “Not scheduled” when there's no such record on file. Health Score is Salesforce's own Health_Score__c field (0-9 scale) taken directly from the Account record — a separate signal from the Risk/Expected Usage columns, which this dashboard derives itself from trailing 30-day page usage. Avg Time/Case divides total hours logged in-app by new cases created, both summed over the trailing 30 days directly from Usage_data__c (Total_Time_spent_in_App_hr__c ÷ Number_of_Cases_Created__c) — a period average, not a per-case timer, shown as N/A when no case was created in the window.

This is a point-in-time snapshot embedded in Salesforce as a Static Resource. The live, auto-refreshing version of this dashboard is published at claude.ai; this copy reflects the data as of the date in the eyebrow above and does not update itself.
