Tracks SiftMed's real, paying customers (Salesforce Account.Stage__c = “Customer”) whose trailing 30-day product usage (Pages_Last_30__c) is low relative to their contracted page cap (PageCountCap__c), prorated to each account's actual contract length (Active_Contract_Start_Date__c to Subscription_End_Date__c) rather than a flat 12-month assumption — the same usage-cliff pattern that preceded all nine of SiftMed's confirmed churns. Accounts missing a start date, end date, or page cap, or with a zero page cap, are excluded from the calculation. Non-paying and internal Account records (null or zero Annual_Contract_Value__c) are excluded from the 53 real-customer base entirely. Usage_data__c is intentionally not used as a source, since it carries rolling placeholder/demo activity rather than real customer signal.
