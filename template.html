Tracks SiftMed's tracked accounts (Salesforce Account.Stage__c ∈ “Customer”, “SQL”, “Prospect”) whose trailing 30-day product usage (Pages_Last_30__c) is low relative to their contracted or projected page cap (PageCountCap__c), prorated to each account's actual contract length (Active_Contract_Start_Date__c to Subscription_End_Date__c) rather than a flat 12-month assumption — the same usage-cliff pattern that preceded all nine of SiftMed's confirmed churns. Accounts missing a start date, end date, or page cap, or with a zero page cap, are excluded from the calculation (shown as N/A). Non-real and internal Account records (null or zero Annual_Contract_Value__c), and accounts at any other stage (Previous Customer, Unqualifed, Kick Back, etc.), are excluded from the __TOTAL_N__ tracked-account base entirely. The Stage column shows the account's tier alongside a Customer-stage account, e.g. “Customer (Enterprise)”; SQL and Prospect accounts are still in the sales pipeline and haven't necessarily signed a contract yet, so their page cap and usage figures may be provisional. Last Email reflects the most recent logged Email-type Task on the Account; Next Meeting reflects the earliest future-dated Event on the Account — both show “Never” / “Not scheduled” when there's no such record on file.

This is a point-in-time snapshot embedded in Salesforce as a Static Resource. The live, auto-refreshing version of this dashboard is published at claude.ai; this copy reflects the data as of the date in the eyebrow above and does not update itself.
